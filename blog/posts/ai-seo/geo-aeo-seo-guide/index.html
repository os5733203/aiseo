What are SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO improves the technical and content foundations of organic search. GEO, or generative engine optimisation, considers how a business is represented in AI-generated answers. AEO, or answer engine optimisation, focuses on clear, helpful answers to customer questions. These are overlapping approaches rather than three independent ranking systems.

Where should a business start?

Start with your actual services and the questions a customer needs answered before enquiring. Check that the relevant pages can be accessed and indexed, that each page has a clear purpose, and that your business details are consistent. An inaccessible service page is not fixed by adding an AI label to it.

Make claims easy to check

Use genuine examples, identified authors and accurate service descriptions. If you publish a result, explain its source, time period and scope. Do not invent reviews or imply that a third-party tool score is a search-engine endorsement. Helpful evidence gives readers a reason to trust the page.

Use structure to explain, not to promise

A direct answer, relevant heading, comparison table or process list can make a page easier to use when that format fits the question. Structured data should describe what is visible. Google does not require special GEO/AEO markup or an llms.txt file for its AI search features; focus on the actual page and its usefulness.

Measure the right things

Use page and query performance, relevant enquiries and conversion checks to review business impact. An AI visibility review can use an agreed set of prompts, recording the platform, date and context. One mention does not establish stable visibility, and a missing mention does not prove the website is technically broken.

Connect the next step

A visitor who understands the answer should be able to explore the relevant service, see who provides it and make an informed enquiry. Link your educational pages to specific services using descriptive text, and link service pages back to useful guidance. Review the site as a connected experience.
