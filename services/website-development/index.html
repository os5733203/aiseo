Website Design & Development

Websites built from scratch, with search in mind.

I plan, design and build responsive websites around your services and customer journeys. Work directly with Omar Salah for a scoped plan and hands-on implementation.

What this service does

I plan, design and build responsive websites around your services and customer journeys. WordPress, Shopify or a custom static website is chosen according to the brief, with SEO foundations considered from the start.

Agree the structure before development

We define the audience, services, essential pages, language requirements, forms and integrations. A page map connects each important search intent to a useful page. The project scope states what content and assets are supplied, what needs creating, and how the website will be maintained after handover.

Design a clear mobile experience

Navigation, readable text, contact forms and service comparisons are designed for real devices. The visual system includes consistent colours, typography and components. Motion is used selectively, with reduced-motion support and content that remains available when animation is disabled.

Build the technical foundations

Clean URLs, unique page metadata, semantic headings, internal links and appropriate structured data are part of the build. Images are sized and compressed, key assets are prioritised, and scripts are kept purposeful. For rebuilds, existing URLs and any required redirects are mapped before launch.

Test forms, journeys and handover

Checks cover responsive layouts, navigation, form behaviour and the agreed tracking events. Booking tools, payments or account systems are scoped individually. You receive the website files or CMS access and a clear record of the implementation; hosting and third-party subscriptions are agreed separately.

Questions before we start

Can you work with my existing website?+

Yes. An initial review identifies what can be improved within your current platform. A rebuild is recommended only when the goals and constraints justify it.

Are rankings or AI citations guaranteed?+

No. Search engines and AI platforms choose what they show. The work focuses on accessible pages, useful content, accurate information and measurable improvements within the agreed scope.

What will I receive?+

The agreed deliverables can include findings, a page or keyword map, implemented changes and a change log. The proposal states the scope, responsibilities and review process.
