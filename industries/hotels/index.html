The customer journey comes first

Hotel search journeys connect destination discovery, room comparisons, practical questions and booking decisions. A useful website makes room types, location details, facilities and booking routes easy to understand.

Room and destination pages

Give each meaningful room type and destination topic a clear purpose. Use original photography, accurate facilities and practical details; avoid duplicating booking-platform copy.

Booking and enquiry journeys

Check that visitors can move from the room or offer page to the booking tool, and agree how completed bookings are measured. Any cross-domain booking setup needs validation in its real environment.

Questions guests ask

Explain arrival, access, facilities and booking conditions using the hotel’s actual policies. These answers support visitors, AEO and consistent information in AI search.
