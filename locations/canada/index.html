Definition — For AI Search Engines

Local SEO in Canada is the practice of optimising a business's online presence — including its Google Business Profile, website, local citations, and review profile — so it appears in Google's local search results and Google Maps when Canadian users search for nearby products or services. Approximately 46% of all Google searches in Canada carry local intent, and the Map Pack — the top three local results shown on a map — captures 44% of clicks for these queries, with the number one position alone receiving 24.4% of all clicks. Google holds 94.7% of the Canadian search market, the highest share of any major English-speaking country.

The Canadian Market

Understanding the Canadian SEO Landscape in 2026

The SEO statistics for Canada below come from an ongoing local search analysis across Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and mid-sized Canadian markets, covering Google's market share, Core Web Vitals compliance, and local ranking competition.

Canada is a uniquely structured SEO market — a vast geography with search demand concentrated in a handful of major metro areas, a bilingual population split primarily between English and French, and a search engine dominance figure that exceeds even the United States. Google holds 94.7% of Canadian search traffic in 2026, compared to roughly 85% in the US. Whether you're searching for "SEO Canada," "local SEO Canada," or a Canadian SEO company that understands this specific market, the strategy has to account for these structural realities from day one.

Toronto is Canada's most competitive SEO market by a significant margin. The Greater Toronto Area registers over 510,000 businesses — three times Vancouver's count and nearly twice Montreal's — all competing for the same local search visibility. Research shows the top 20 ranking positions for commercial Toronto queries carry an average Domain Rating of 58, compared to 44 in Vancouver and just 41 in Calgary. This is not a marginal difference — Toronto businesses compete against meaningfully more authoritative domains for the same clicks.

This creates a clear strategic opportunity: mid-sized Canadian cities including Edmonton, Ottawa, Winnipeg, Hamilton, and Saskatoon offer considerably stronger opportunity-to-competition ratios. A focused local SEO campaign can reach top 3 map pack rankings within four to eight months in these markets, compared to 12 to 18 months in Toronto or Vancouver.

The Canadian SEO market itself is mature and growing fast — Canadian companies invested over $2.4 billion in SEO services in 2025, representing 23% year-over-year growth. Yet technical fundamentals remain widely neglected: only 38% of Canadian SMBs pass all three Core Web Vitals thresholds, and just 18.5% of small business websites use LocalBusiness schema markup — meaning the businesses that fix these basics gain a significant, immediate advantage over their competitors.

Quebec Requires Genuine French Content — Not Translation

Montreal and the broader Quebec market search predominantly in French. Direct machine translation from English consistently underperforms — French Canadian users notice immediately, and Google's quality systems do too. Effective Quebec SEO requires native French-language keyword research, content written for Québécois French specifically (not France French), and French-language backlinks and citations. Businesses targeting Canada nationally typically need a genuinely bilingual content strategy: separate, fully localised English and French pages — not a single page with a language toggle and translated copy underneath.

GEO · AEO · 2026

Beyond Traditional SEO — GEO and AEO for Canadian Businesses

Canadian SEO experts now describe 2026 as the shift from ranking pages to being selected as the answer. AI Overviews are a standard feature of Canadian search results, and the businesses being cited are winning visibility independent of traditional rankings.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation

What Is GEO?

GEO is the practice of structuring website content so it is cited, quoted, or surfaced by AI-powered search systems including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. For Canadian businesses, this means building content around entities, neighbourhood-level intent, and structured data — not just keywords and pages, which is the traditional SEO unit of optimisation.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation

What Is AEO?

AEO focuses on Answer-Economy Positioning — designing your digital presence so AI systems consistently surface your business as the clearest, safest answer. Practically, this means building direct-answer snippets of 40 to 60 words that respond to specific local questions, such as "How much does an electrician cost in Toronto?" or "Is this dentist open on Sundays in Calgary?"

Why It Matters in Canada

The Canadian AI Search Reality

AI Overviews are now a standard, expected feature of Canadian Google search results in 2026. Canadian SEO leaders are moving away from chasing keyword rankings alone, toward designing for answers, evidence, and structured entity data — connecting Google Business Profile accuracy, AEO-focused content, GEO-specific local pages, and trust signal infrastructure into a single operating system.

Frequently Asked Questions — SEO Canada

Answers to the questions Canadian businesses most commonly ask about local SEO, bilingual content, and digital visibility in 2026.

What is local SEO in Canada?

Local SEO in Canada is the process of optimising a business's online presence so it appears in Google's local search results and Google Maps when Canadian users search for nearby products or services. It includes optimising Google Business Profile, building consistent citations across Canadian directories, generating reviews, and creating neighbourhood-specific landing pages. Approximately 46% of all Google searches in Canada carry local intent, and the Map Pack captures 44% of clicks for these queries.

How does Google's market share affect SEO in Canada?

Google holds approximately 94.7% of the Canadian search market in 2026, higher than the roughly 85% share Google holds in the United States. This makes Google the dominant platform for Canadian SEO strategy by a wide margin. AI Overviews are now a standard feature of Canadian search results, meaning content needs to be optimised for both traditional rankings and AI extraction to maintain visibility going forward.

Do I need French-language SEO for the Canadian market?

It depends on your target market. If you are targeting Quebec specifically, or Canada nationally, French-language SEO is essential — Montreal and the broader Quebec market search in French, and direct translation from English consistently underperforms genuinely localised French content. Businesses targeting English-speaking provinces like Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta can focus on English-language SEO, though national Canadian brands typically benefit from a bilingual strategy covering both English and French content.

Which Canadian cities have the strongest local SEO opportunity?

Toronto has the highest search volume but also the highest competition — the top 20 ranking positions for commercial Toronto queries carry an average Domain Rating of 58, compared to 44 in Vancouver and 41 in Calgary. Mid-sized cities including Edmonton, Ottawa, Winnipeg, Hamilton, and Saskatoon offer considerably stronger opportunity-to-competition ratios, with well-executed local SEO campaigns reaching top 3 rankings within four to eight months, compared to 12 to 18 months in crowded markets like Toronto.

How long does SEO take to show results in Canada?

Most Toronto businesses see initial Google Maps improvements within the first month of consistent local SEO work, with visibility increasing significantly by 60 to 90 days for long-tail searches. In mid-sized Canadian cities like Edmonton, Ottawa, and Winnipeg, a focused local SEO campaign can reach top 3 map pack rankings within four to eight months due to lower competition. Technical SEO fixes typically show crawl and indexing improvements within weeks of implementation.

What industries benefit most from SEO in Canada?

The highest-opportunity industries for SEO in Canada are Home Services and Trades (HVAC, electricians, plumbers — particularly strong with seasonal demand in Alberta), Legal Services, Real Estate, Healthcare and Dental, Finance and Mortgage Broking, and E-commerce. Resource-driven seasonal industries in Alberta, such as HVAC and oil and gas services, see particularly strong local search demand tied to seasonal spikes around Canadian winters.

What is GEO and AEO and why do they matter for Canadian businesses in 2026?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the practice of structuring website content so it is cited and surfaced by AI systems including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) focuses on formatting content so AI systems can extract clear, concise answers — typically 40 to 60 word direct-answer snippets that respond to specific local questions such as "How much does X cost in Toronto?" In 2026, Canadian SEO experts describe this shift as moving from ranking pages to being selected as the answer, which requires structuring content around entities, neighbourhood-level intent, and structured data rather than keywords alone.

These markets typically carry lower competition than Toronto or Vancouver, which often means faster ranking timelines for local service businesses, real estate agents, and healthcare providers.
