FAQ

Before we begin.

Can you build my website from scratch?+

Yes. I can plan, design and build a website around your services, audience and goals. We agree the platform, content, scope and integrations before development starts.

How do SEO, GEO and AEO work together?+

SEO improves the foundations for organic discovery. GEO focuses on how generative search understands your business, while AEO structures useful answers to real questions. Clear content, accessible pages and trustworthy evidence support all three; rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.

How will we measure progress?+

We agree a baseline and relevant measures: search impressions and clicks, qualified enquiries, website performance and completed actions. AI visibility can be checked against a defined set of questions, with its limitations explained.
